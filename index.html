Micro Perform Part 1

Artist statement

For part 1 of this assignment, I used the Raspbperry Pi Pico and a breadboard to create a micro performance of Led lights, sounds, 3D-printed buttons, and interactive elements. This project was more of a test before the final part, which proved to be quite a challenge. I have never previously worked with breadboards or a 3D printer, so every step of the process was a learning experience filled with trial and error. The most challenging parts for me was printing the keycaps successfully, as well as getting the wiring to work properly. This was an extremely long and challenging process, but I am proud with the fact that I got everything working properly and even was able to do the bonus "Beat It" sequence.
